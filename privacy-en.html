Privacy Policy

Prologue · Last updated: September 30, 2026

Who we are

Prologue is an app for tracking TV shows and movies: you log what you watch, check off episodes and find out what to watch next.

The controller of your personal data is XF Corp. You can contact them by email at xfcorpz@gmail.com.

The data we collect

We only collect what the app needs to work:

  • Your account: your email address (or, if you sign in with Apple, Google or Facebook, the unique identifier and email address that service sends us — with Apple's “Hide My Email” we only receive a relay address). Your password, if you use one, is never readable: it is protected by our authentication service.
  • Your content: the shows and movies you follow, the episodes you mark as watched (with the date), your ratings and your reactions.
  • Your profile (optional): the username, profile photo and banner you choose yourself, and your privacy settings.
  • Your life in the app: your lists and favorites, your followers and the people you follow, the comments you post, as well as your reports and the people you block.
  • Your messages to us: if you use “Send feedback” in the settings, the subject and message you write, your account's email address (so we can reply) and, if you agree, the app version, phone type and language. They are kept for as long as needed to handle your request.
  • Technical data: if crash reports are turned on, an anonymous report when the app crashes (device model, system and app version, a technical description of the error, a technical identifier of your account). Our server logs also record your IP address.
  • Your preferences (theme, language, region, notifications): they stay stored on your device.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no advertising trackers, no selling of data.
  • We don't access your location, your contacts or your photo library: only the photo you choose yourself as your profile picture is uploaded.
  • We don't track what you do in other apps.

Why we use your data

  • To provide the service (account, lists, history, statistics, syncing between your devices) — performance of the contract.
  • To let you know when an episode or movie you follow is released — your consent, through your phone's notification permission, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • To fix bugs and keep the app secure (crash reports, logs) — legitimate interest.
  • To show aggregated community statistics (how many people follow a title, breakdown of ratings and reactions) — legitimate interest.
  • To provide the social features you choose to use (followers, shared activity, comments) and to moderate reported content — performance of the contract and legitimate interest.

What other users can see

Nobody can see your email address or your individual ratings. Here is what is visible, and to whom:

  • Everyone: anonymous totals (for example “1,200 people follow this show”), and the comments you post, with your username, photo and banner.
  • Other users who search for your username: your username, photo and banner, unless you turn off “Appear in search”.
  • Your followers: the episodes and movies you watch (last 30 days) and simple counters, unless you turn off “Share my activity with followers”. They also see the lists you choose to share with them. Your username, photo, banner and your number of followers and people you follow are visible to other users; you can hide yourself from search by turning off “Appear in search”.
  • Nobody else: your other lists stay private.

Who we share data with

We use service providers, who only use your data to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase — hosting of the database and profile photos, and authentication.
  • TMDB (The Movie Database) — details, posters, casts, trends. Searches and title lookups are sent from your device to TMDB, which therefore receives your IP address.
  • JustWatch (via TMDB) — availability of titles on streaming platforms.
  • OMDb — external ratings (IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes, Metacritic): receives the IMDb identifier of the title you are viewing.
  • Sentry — receives anonymous crash reports, unless you turned them off in Settings.
  • Apple, Google or Facebook — only if you choose to sign in with one of them.

Transfers outside the European Union

Some providers may process data outside the European Union (notably in the United States). These transfers are covered by the mechanisms provided for by the GDPR: an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Your account data (including profile, photo, lists, followers and comments): for as long as your account exists. It is deleted immediately when you delete your account; technical backups are purged automatically at the end of their rotation cycle.
  • Content reports: as long as needed to handle them.
  • Crash reports: 90 days at most.
  • Technical server logs: the short period set by our host.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to and port your data, as well as the right to withdraw your consent.

Directly in the app, from Settings: “Export my data” (a JSON file of everything we hold about you) and “Delete my account” (permanent erasure).

For any other request, write to us by email at xfcorpz@gmail.com. If you believe your rights are not being respected, you can lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in France, the CNIL — cnil.fr).

Security

Exchanges between the app and our servers are encrypted (HTTPS). Access to data is restricted to your own account. As no system is infallible, we will notify you of any breach affecting your data, as required by law.

Minors

Prologue is intended for people aged 15 and over. Below that age, the consent of a parent or legal guardian is required.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If there is an important change, we will let you know in the app. Last updated: September 30, 2026.
