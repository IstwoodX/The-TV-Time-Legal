What the app does

Tracking in one tap

One tap to check off the next episode, a season or a whole series. Your progress is always up to date.

Release calendar

Upcoming episodes and movies, day by day. Reminders on release day, and even in your phone's calendar.

Bring your history

From TV Time, Trakt, IMDb, Letterboxd and more: followed shows, watched episodes, movies, ratings.

For you

Suggestions based on what you watch, refreshed every day. Or swipe through cards to fill your list.

Lists, even shared ones

Create private or shared lists. Invite a friend to fill them together.

Community

Follow friends, comment on episodes, react with a GIF. You choose what is visible.

Where to watch

The streaming services offering each title in your country, one tap to open the app.

Made for your phone

Widgets, dark mode, offline reading, 12 languages that follow your phone.

Your privacy matters

No ads, no selling your data. You decide whether your activity is visible, and you can export all your data or delete your account at any time from the app. Read the privacy policy.

A question, an idea, a bug?

In the app: Settings › Send feedback. Or visit the help page.
