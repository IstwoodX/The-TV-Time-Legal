Delete my account and data

You can delete your account at any time. It is immediate and permanent.

From the app (the fastest way)

  1. Open The TV Time and go to Settings.
  2. At the bottom, in the Account section, tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and all your data are erased.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in methods (email, Apple, Google, Facebook).
  • Your followed shows and movies, watched episodes, ratings and reactions.
  • Your profile: username, photo and banner.
  • Your lists, favorites, followers and following, comments and reports.
  • Messages sent through “Send feedback” and the devices registered for notifications.

Technical backups on our servers are purged automatically at the end of their rotation cycle. Anonymous crash reports contain no personal data.

Keep a copy first

Settings › Export my data generates a file with everything your account contains.

Can't open the app?

Write to us from your account's email address at xfcorpz@gmail.com with “Delete my account”. We process the request within one month at most.
