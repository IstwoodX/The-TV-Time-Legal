Help

Answers to the most common questions. Need anything else? Write to us: xfcorpz@gmail.com.

How do I import my history from another app?
  1. Export your data from the other app (TV Time, Trakt, IMDb, Letterboxd…).
  2. In The TV Time: Settings › Import my history.
  3. Pick the file (even a .zip, with its password if needed). The app shows you what it found before saving anything.

You can run an import again without creating duplicates.

Some of my shows or titles are missing or weren't recognized.

The import matches titles by name and year. Those that couldn't be matched are listed at the end. If TMDB didn't answer, simply run the import again: it picks up what was missing.

How do I change my email or password?

Settings › My account. For your security, a confirmation email is sent: the change only takes effect after you tap the link in it. Check your spam folder.

I didn't receive the confirmation email.

Check your spam folder, then use “Resend the confirmation email” on the sign-in screen (a short delay applies between two sends). If nothing arrives, write to us.

How do I add releases to my phone's calendar?

Settings › Calendar › Add releases to my calendar, then allow access. A “The TV Time” calendar appears and stays up to date automatically. You can choose shows, movies, or both.

How do I invite someone to a list?

Open the list and tap the “invite” icon. You can invite people you follow who also follow you. They receive the invitation in their notification bell.

Where does the show and movie information come from?

The information comes from TMDB, and streaming availability from JustWatch (via TMDB). The TV Time is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

How do I report a problem or suggest an idea?

Settings › About › Send feedback: choose Problem, Idea or Feedback, write a subject and a message. You can include the app version and phone type to help us understand.

How do I delete my account?

Right in the app, in a few seconds: see Delete my account and data.
